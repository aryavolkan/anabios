Chapter 00 · The Cradle A founding population wakes in the savanna. They forage, cluster, and die young. Everything that follows is already latent in fifty numbers per genome — it just hasn't happened yet.

Chapter 01 · Tools & Fire The first inventions surface — techniques a lineage teaches rather than inherits. Watch the codex log each one the moment it is discovered and then adopted.

Chapter 02 · The Exodus The cradle fills; pressure pushes lineages across the torus. Isolation does the rest — dialects drift and populations split until the detectors fire speciation.

Chapter 03 · Farming & Trade Some settle. Surplus invents the first economy — settlements, markets, and goods carried between distant bands in threads of grain, hide, and stone.

Chapter 04 · Writing Culture stops decaying between generations and begins to accumulate. Meme sweeps and cultural radiation ripple across the map faster than any single life could carry them.

Chapter 05 · War & Kin Scarcity finds the fault lines. Neighbours who share no kin-mark clash at the borders — and behind each front, the tightest kin-bands survive to seed the next age.
